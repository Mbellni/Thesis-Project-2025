# Thesis-Project-2025

This repository contains the full development, documentation, and research materials for my 2025 Master’s thesis project.  
The project explores **AI-driven persuasive behaviour in VR**, using an interactive **AI NPC** (Convai + Unreal Engine) to study whether short, immersive conversational experiences can influence users' beliefs about ambiguous statements.

Users interact with a human-like virtual agent inside VR, answer pre- and post-interaction questionnaires, and the system analyses belief change, presence, trust, and credibility.

---

## Project Overview

The goal of this project is to examine **how immersive environments and AI-driven dialogue influence user beliefs**.  
The system combines:

- **Unreal Engine VR experience** featuring a MetaHuman NPC  
- **Convai dialogue system** for real-time AI conversation  

---

## Features

- **VR Interaction System**  
  Fully interactive VR scenario built in Unreal Engine.

- **AI-Driven NPC (Convai)**  
  Natural conversational responses, persuasive dialogue behaviour, and real-time language model reasoning.

- **Belief-Change Measurement**  
  Users rate the likelihood of an ambiguous claim before and after the VR session.

- **Progress Journals**  
  Weekly journals documenting design, implementation, setbacks, iterations, and academic decisions.

##  How to Run the Experiment

### 1. Connect your Meta Quest to your PC
- Open the **Meta Quest Link** app.  
- Plug in your headset via USB-C or enable **Air Link**.  
- Accept the “Enable Link Mode” prompt inside the headset.

---

### 2. Launch the Experience
1. On your PC, open the folder you extracted from OneDrive.  
2. Double-click the application file
3. Put on your headset — you should automatically be inside the VR environment.

---

##  How to Interact With the AI Character

### **Enable Push-to-Talk (A button)**

Your character speaks using a push-to-talk system:

- **Hold the A button** on the **right-hand controller** to talk.  
- **Release the A button** when you finish speaking.  
- The AI NPC will reply after a short moment.

### Tips for best experience

- Sit down during the experiment (recommended).  
- Speak clearly and at a normal pace.  
- Keep the microphone close (don’t cover the controller).  
- Wait for the NPC to finish speaking before pressing A again.

---

##  Additional Notes

- If audio cuts or lags, ensure the Quest Link connection is stable.  
- If the environment appears blurry, increase the render resolution in the Oculus PC app.
- If the sound or mic is not working, make sure to check the sound settings on your PC
- You can exit the experiment anytime by taking off the headset and closing the app on your PC.
